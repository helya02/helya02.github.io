I am an M.Sc. student in Computer Engineering at Bilkent University, advised by Dr. A. Ercüment Çiçek. I develop machine learning methods for RNA design, including generative models that design RNA to bind proteins and small molecules, and RNA–protein interface predictors that generalise beyond biased structural benchmarks.

Before Bilkent, I completed my B.Sc. in Computer Engineering at the University of Tabriz. I am applying to Ph.D. programmes starting in 2027.
