What this does

Before you simulate. It reads the complex, tells you what will break your topology build (gaps, missing side chains, non-standard residues, clashes), measures the protein–RNA interface, works out the box, water count, neutralising ions and salt, and writes ready-to-run GROMACS, AMBER and OpenMM inputs with force fields that are appropriate for RNA.

After you simulate. Load a multi-model PDB trajectory and it computes RMSD, per-residue RMSF, radius of gyration, interface contact and hydrogen-bond occupancy, base-pair lifetimes and any distance you want to track — all exportable as CSV.

It does not run the simulationMolecular dynamics needs a real engine on real hardware. This is the workbench around it: preparation, decisions, input files and analysis. Everything runs in your browser — no file ever leaves your machine.

Collective motions elastic network model

Every residue is a node, every close pair a spring. Diagonalising that network gives the slow collective motions directly, with no sampling. The test of whether it means anything is the crystallographic B-factors, which the model never saw.
